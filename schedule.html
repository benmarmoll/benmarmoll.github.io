Friday, October 9, 2026

5:30 pm – 6:45 pm

Welcome Hike

The Lake Trahlyta Trail

Vogel State Park · 405 Vogel State Park Rd, Blairsville, GA 30512

The Lake Trahlyta Trail in Vogel State Park is an easy 1-mile loop featuring stunning mountain lake views and a short spur trail leading to the 60-foot Trahlyta Falls. We will provide trail snacks and water, but please plan to eat prior to or after the event, as food will not be provided. We would love to say hello and do one of our favorite activities with all who wish to attend before the Saturday festivities!
