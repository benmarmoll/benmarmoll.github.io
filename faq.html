FAQs

Question

Where is the ceremony?

Our wedding ceremony will take place at the beautiful Lakeside Pavilion at Vogel State Park. We look forward to celebrating with you in this special setting!

Question

What time should I arrive for the ceremony?

We kindly ask that you arrive early for the ceremony. This will allow ample time to find your seat and get settled before the proceedings begin. Please reference the Schedule page for timing details!

Question

Will transportation be provided to the ceremony?

There will be a shuttle from the hotel to and from the Ceremony/Reception. Please arrive on time as the shuttle will leave promptly.

Question

Should I rent a car, or is transportation easily accessible in the area?

For ease of travel, we recommend renting a car. Public transportation options are limited in the area, and a car will allow you greater flexibility.

Question

Is there parking for the ceremony and reception?

Parking at Vogel State Park is $10 per car. You can reserve parking in advance on their website or at the gate. The parking lot is located near the pavilion for easy access. The ceremony and event room are located within a short walk from each other.

Question

What should I wear? What is the dress code?

Cocktail Casual! Wear what you want, it is a state park wedding after all!

Question

Are kids welcome?

Yes! There will be games and favors to keep your little ones entertained!

Question

How do I know if I can bring a plus-one?

All plus-ones are specified on your invitation. We've thoughtfully planned our guest list, so we kindly ask that you RSVP only for those listed on the invitation. Please contact the Bride or Groom directly with any questions about this!

Question

Will there be meal options for guests with dietary restrictions?

Yes, please inform us of any dietary restrictions or allergies with your RSVP, and we will gladly accommodate. We are planning to serve Barbecue buffet style and desserts made by the Bride!

Question

What should I do while I'm in town?

While in town, we encourage you to explore the local charm. Consider walking through town and stopping at one of the cafes! We suggest hiking Brasstown Bald for beautiful views!
