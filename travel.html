Getting There

Travel

Hotel

Best Western Milton Inn

201 Highway 515 West, Blairsville, GA 30512

We have blocked a group of rooms at the hotel for your convenience. If you wish to reserve a room in our hotel block, please call the hotel directly at 706-745-6995 and say you are with the Vallone/Marmoll wedding. Book by September 8th for the discounted rate.

If you wish to stay elsewhere, there are multiple other hotels and Airbnbs nearby to choose from. Paradise Hills Winery Resort is another great option located about 10 minutes from Vogel State Park. You can check their website for availability at: https://www.paradisehillsga.com/. This is a rural town and options are limited, so please book in advance.
